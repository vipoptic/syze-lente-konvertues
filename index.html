This calculation is based on the eyeglass prescription provided and the standard vertex-distance correction formula (Flens = Fspectacle / (1 − d × Fspectacle)). Since the result is theoretical, the exact contact lens prescription should be determined by a qualified optometrist or ophthalmologist during an eye examination.

Sphere: rounded to the nearest available step of 0.25D (up to ±6.00D) and 0.50D (beyond ±6.00D). Cylinder: rounded to the nearest real in-stock toric value (0, -0.75, -1.25, -1.75, -2.25, -2.75, -3.25, -3.75, -4.25, -4.75, -5.25, -5.75). Axis is rounded according to the recommended brand's step (10° for most, 5° for Biofinity XR Toric, and only specific angles 10/20/70/80/90/100/160/170/180° for TopVue Daily for Astigmatism).

Priority order depends on the "Wear type" (First-time wearer / Extended wear / Budget) and "Replacement schedule" (Monthly / Daily) selected above, based on the shop's "Sygjerimet" spreadsheet. The tool always gives up to 2 alternatives, and applies the shop's rules: (a) if the cylinder forces one eye into a wider-range brand, the other eye gets the same brand; (b) if one eye is toric and the other spherical, the spherical brand is suggested from the same family as the chosen toric brand; (c) for monthly lenses, if one eye only fits Biofinity XR Toric, the other eye gets Biofinity Toric if its range allows.
